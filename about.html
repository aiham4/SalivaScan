About the project

SalivaScan is a student research prototype built for the 4CBLW010 Multi-Disciplinary CBL course at TU/e. It explores whether Type 2 diabetes can be screened non-invasively from ATR-FTIR saliva spectra, with all analysis running privately in your browser.
